01

Meet & plan

We meet online or in person and talk through what you want the site to change — the goals, the pages, the tone. Within two business days you receive a sample format: a first version of the design, on your content, to react to.

You get: a sample format of your site within 2 business days.
2business days
02

Build & test

We build the full site from the agreed sample and run test runs together: every page, every screen size, every link and form. You see it on a private preview link the whole way.

You get: the finished site on a preview link, tested with you.
1–2business days
03

Connect & launch

Your domain is connected, the site is published from your own GitHub repository with SSL, and you get a short walkthrough on how updates work from here on.

You get: your site live on your domain, source in your repository.
1business day
