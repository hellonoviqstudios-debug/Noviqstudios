FAQ

Beforeyou ask.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most. Anything else — ask the assistant in the corner, or email us.

How long does it take?

About one week. We meet and agree the aim, you get a sample format within two business days, building and test runs take one to two business days, and connecting and launching takes one business day. Your website is reborn in a week.

Do I need to write my own text?

No. Send what you have — old brochures, a paragraph in an email, your current site — and we draft the copy for you to correct.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, the content you already have and what the site needs to do. Fill in the proposal form and you'll get a written proposal with a fixed price within two business days — no obligation.

What if I already have a website?

We rebuild it around your real content and photography rather than patching it, and we keep every page address that already ranks in Google so you don't lose traffic in the switch.

How do updates work after launch?

Send us the change — new text, photos, opening hours — and we publish it. Because the site lives in your GitHub repository, every version is kept and any change can be rolled back.

Where is the site hosted?

On GitHub Pages, under your own domain, with free SSL. There is no monthly platform fee for hosting, and the source code stays in a repository you own.

Can you build a web shop or booking system?

Not ourselves — we keep to fast static sites. We can build a site or landing page that links to an external booking or shop tool, and we'll tell you honestly what fits before you commit.

Do you work with businesses outside the Netherlands?

Yes. Most of the work happens over shared links and short calls, in English or Dutch, across time zones.

What do you need from me to start?

The proposal form, then a meeting — online or in person — plus your logo and any photos you have. Everything else we work out as we go.

Still have a question?

The assistant in the bottom corner answers in seconds. Or email hellonoviqstudios@gmail.com — we reply within one business day.
