CookiePolicy.

What this site stores in your browser, and what happens if that ever changes.

Last updated: 14 September 2026

1. Short answer

This Site does not currently set any cookies, and does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other analytics or advertising tracker. Nothing on this Site tracks you across visits or across other websites today.

2. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file a website can ask your browser to store, often used to remember who you are between visits or to track browsing behaviour. Websites can also use similar browser storage (such as "local storage") for the same purposes. Under Dutch and EU law (the ePrivacy rules, alongside the GDPR), a site generally needs your permission before it sets any cookie that isn't strictly necessary for the site to function — which is why, if we ever add one, we'll ask first.

3. What this Site actually uses

  • Fonts, styles and scripts are all self-hosted — loading a page here doesn't send a request to Google Fonts or any other third-party CDN.
  • The chat widget, where available, sends your typed question to Anthropic's Claude service using your own browser session to generate a reply; this is a live request, not a stored cookie, and is covered in the Privacy Policy.
  • No analytics and no advertising trackers are active on this Site as of the date above.
If this changes: we're planning to add basic analytics in the future to understand how the Site is used. When we do, a consent banner will appear before any analytics cookie is set, giving you the choice to accept or decline — nothing non-essential will run until you say yes. This page will be updated at the same time to name the specific tool and what it collects.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

Even though this Site doesn't set any today, your browser lets you view, block or delete cookies from any site at any time — check your browser's settings or help pages for how.
